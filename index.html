🔍 How to Spot AI Images

👋 Hands & fingers: Check shapes, joints and object interactions.

🔤 Text & signs: Look for distorted or meaningless lettering.

👀 Faces & details: Look closely at hair, eyes and fine details.

🪞 Reflections & shadows: Check whether lighting and reflections make sense.

🏙️ Background: Look for merged, repeated or inconsistent objects.

🔎 Source: When authenticity matters, check where the image came from.
