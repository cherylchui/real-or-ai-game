🔍 HOW TO ASSESS AN IMAGE

👋
Hands & Fingers

Check finger shapes, joints and how hands interact with objects.

🔤
Text & Signs

Look for distorted, meaningless or inconsistent lettering.

👀
Faces & Details

Look closely at hair, fine details and unusual visual inconsistencies.

🪞
Reflections & Shadows

Check whether lighting, reflections and shadows make physical sense.

🏙️
Background Details

Look for merged objects, repeated patterns or impossible geometry.

🔎
Source & Context

When authenticity matters, check where the image came from rather than relying only on visual clues.

AI images are becoming increasingly realistic. Look carefully, think critically and check the source.
